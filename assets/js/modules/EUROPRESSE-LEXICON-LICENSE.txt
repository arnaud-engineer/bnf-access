Changes: selected vowel/h-initial nouns and verbs with frequency >= 5 per million
in either Lexique corpus; folded accents and ligatures for lookup; retained intact
words which could otherwise collide with an elided candidate. Sorted compact
word lists generated by private/scripts/build_europresse_elision_lexicon.py.
This is a limited lookup aid, not a complete French dictionary or a proper-name
database. Unknown words must not be treated as incorrect.
